# Carolina Data Advisory — 5-Minute Client CSV Export Cheat Sheet

Send these three standard **CSV (or Excel)** exports to begin your 48-hour dashboard build. No IT integration or software login is required.

## The 3 Standard Exports (Any Software Platform)
1. **Master Customer / Account / Patient List:** ID, Name, Segment/Plan Type, Account Owner/Provider, and City/Territory.
2. **36-Month Transaction / Invoice / Job History:** Transaction ID, Customer ID, Date, Service/Product Category, Gross Revenue, Gross Margin (or Cost), and Payment Status.
3. **Open Pipeline & Aging Action Items:**
   * Open/unclosed quotes, estimates, or consultations older than 7 days.
   * Aging Accounts Receivable (30/60/90+ days past due) or expiring annual plans/retainers.

---

## Quick Export Paths by Common Platform
* **QuickBooks Online / Desktop:** *Reports > Sales by Customer Detail (Last 36 Months) > Export to Excel/CSV* + *Reports > Open Invoices & Unbilled Charges*.
* **ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber:** *Reports > Jobs & Invoices (36 Months)* + *Estimates Report (Open/Unscheduled)* + *Memberships Expiring Next 60 Days*.
* **Clio / Smokeball / Tabs3 (Legal):** *Reports > Matter Productivity & Realization* + *Accounts Receivable Aging* + *Trust Account Balances*.
* **Dentrix / Eaglesoft / Open Dental:** *Practice Advisor / Production & Collection Summary (36 Months)* + *Unscheduled Treatment Plans* + *Insurance Aging (>45 Days)*.
* **CDK / VinSolutions / Reynolds (Auto):** *DMS Sales & Fixed-Ops RO History (36 Months)* + *Used Inventory Aging (>30 Days)* + *Declined MPI Recommendations*.
* **JobBOSS / E2 / ERP (Manufacturing & Distribution):** *Order/Job Costing History (Quoted vs. Actual Hours/Material)* + *Open RFQ Log* + *AR Aging Summary*.
