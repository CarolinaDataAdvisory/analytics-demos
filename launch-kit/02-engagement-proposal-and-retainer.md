## 1. Objective & Scope of Services
Carolina Data Advisory will consolidate, deduplicate, and standardize [Client Company Name]'s historical billing, CRM, and operational exports into an executive-ready **Profit Recovery & Forecasting Dashboard**—paired with ongoing monthly refreshes and executive advisory calls to track verified ROI.

### Phase 1: Initial Data Hygiene & Executive Dashboard Build (48-Hour Turnaround Upon Data Receipt)
* **Data Standardization:** Deduplicate customer/account records and standardize up to 36 months of historical revenue, margin, and pipeline data.
* **Custom Executive Dashboard:** Interactive tracking of monthly actuals, 6-month baseline vs. outreach forecasts, customer/account LTV multipliers, lapsed-account win-back watchlists, and prioritized daily/weekly Action Queues.
* **Executive Readout & Action Playbook:** 60-minute leadership walkthrough detailing the Top 3 immediate profit-recovery actions.

### Phase 2: Ongoing Monthly Intelligence & Advisory Retainer (12-Month Term)
* **Monthly Data Refresh & Verified ROI Tracking:** Re-run updated monthly CSV exports, refresh forecasts and Action Queues, and report **Cumulative Verified Cash Recovered** since launch.
* **Monthly Executive Strategy & Q&A Call (45 Minutes):** Review prior-month wins, answer leadership ad-hoc data questions, and adjust priorities for the upcoming 30 days.
